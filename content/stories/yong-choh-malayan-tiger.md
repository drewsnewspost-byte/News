Malaysia’s NWRC in Sungkai is preparing to send a wild-caught female Malayan tiger, Yong Choh, back into the forest — the first such release from the centre. Captured by Perhilitan on 22 July in Sungai Choh, Ulu Kinta. After a 21 August visit, Minister Datuk Seri Arthur Joseph Kurup said she is healthy and “sufficiently aggressive to survive independently,” with a planned release in early September (not completed). MyBIT has produced 13 cubs, nine still growing; those cubs wait years. Yong Choh is a recently captured wild adult.
