What happened

Coyote vs. Acme is in wide release as of Friday, Aug. 28, and the Rotten Tomatoes page for the film, checked Sunday morning, prints a 95% Tomatometer from 199 reviews and a 94% audience score on the Popcornmeter from 500-plus verified ratings. Those are the numbers as the site displays them. The critics consensus, copied as RT prints it, reads: “A daffy satire that's equal parts heart and wily humor, Coyote vs. Acme makes a triumphant case for the Looney Tunes' modern relevance with a sense of good-natured mischief that'd make even Bugs Bunny bow.” The page lists the movie as PG, 1 hour 41 minutes, in Kids & Family, Comedy, Adventure, and Animation, now playing, directed by Dave Green, written by Samy Burch, produced by Christopher DeFaria and James Gunn, and distributed by Ketchup Entertainment.

Rotten Tomatoes’ own synopsis is the plot we are sticking to. After decades of being blown up, flattened, tricked, and hurled off cliffs, Wile E. Coyote (Genius) teams with billboard accident lawyer Kevin Avery, played by Will Forte, and takes on slick corporate counsel Buddy Crane, played by John Cena, plus ACME, Inc., the outfit behind the gadgets that keep failing him. That is the lawsuit comedy the scores are attached to. The page also surfaces critic blurbs that stay inside the same lane: Adam Nayman at The Ringer calls the satire matched by craftsmanship and affection for the characters; Thelma Adams at AARP Movies for Grownups writes that not since Who Framed Roger Rabbit in 1988 has there been such a funny, shrewd, and entertaining mix of live action and animation; Andrew Parker at The Gate calls it one of the funniest films of the year and the best of its kind since Roger Rabbit. Audience notes on the same page run from “so emotional with so much Heart” to people who liked the cartoon reels more than the human ones. We are not inventing a score or a consensus line. Those two percentages and that consensus sentence are what RT is showing.
