What happened

National Geographic reports that Bianfu Cave in Yunnan has produced the first Denisovan forearm bone — a radius — along with skull fragments and teeth, identified through protein collagen analysis rather than DNA. The human bones date to roughly 167,000–134,000 years ago, while evidence of site use spans about 190,000–70,000 years ago. Excavators also recovered more than 1,360 stone tools and roughly 60,000 mammal bone fragments. Two Nature papers dated September 9, 2026, carry the scientific results. Ignore wrong paper years or DNA claims that may appear inside comic art; the verified ID method is collagen protein, not DNA.
