What happened

Just Jared’s Sept. 18 entertainment wire says Netflix unveiled the first teaser for Steps, an animated feature from the producers of KPop Demon Hunters. The logline flips the usual fairy-tale blame: Lilith (voiced by Ali Wong), sick of living in Cinderella’s shadow, steals the Fairy Godmother’s wand with sister Margot, hijacks the Royal Ball, and accidentally dooms the kingdom to schemer Priscilla — then must team with Cinderella to fight biker trolls, outrun henchmen, and escape the Screaming Woods. That is the Culture filing: a trailer drop, a cast roll call, and a November date.

The voice roster also includes Amanda Seyfried as Cinderella, Stephanie Hsu as Margot, Nikki Glaser as Priscilla, Daniel Radcliffe as the Prince, Young Mazino as Gef the hot troll, Peter Dinklage as Roderick, Bette Midler as Fairy Godmother, and Damian Priest as Cracktor. The teaser is set to Charli XCX’s “Mean Girls.” Steps debuts on Netflix November 20, 2026. Candy-pink and midnight-ball gold, white gutters, cartoon fairy-tale figures only — never likenesses of living performers.
