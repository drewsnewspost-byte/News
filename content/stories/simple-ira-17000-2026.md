What happened

The IRS retirement-topics page for SIMPLE IRA contribution limits lists the 2026 employee salary reduction (elective deferral) limit at $17,000. That figure is a published ceiling for eligible participants in a Savings Incentive Match Plan for Employees of Small Employers — not a recommendation to contribute any amount, not a product pitch, and not personalized tax or retirement advice. Educational finance only.

On the same IRS board, the age 50+ catch-up contribution for 2026 is printed at $4,000, and the higher catch-up for ages 60–63 under SECURE 2.0 is printed at $5,250. Compensation taken into account for nonelective contributions is capped at $360,000 for 2026. Those lines sit beside the broader COLA and workplace ceilings already filed in prior packages; today’s board is the SIMPLE IRA salary-reduction / catch-up / higher-catch-up / nonelective-compensation quartet most small-business savers actually see on a plan summary.
