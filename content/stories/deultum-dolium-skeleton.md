What happened

Live Science reports that archaeologists at the ancient Roman colony of Deultum in Bulgaria found a roughly 1,600-year-old skeleton inside a giant ceramic storage jar — a dolium — in a Late Roman turret, dated around AD 400. Ash lay under the remains; two iron knives were recovered; and fire evidence surrounds the context. Lyudmil Vagalinski of the Bulgarian Academy of Sciences is among the researchers discussing the find. One working interpretation floated in coverage is that a person may have hidden in the jar during an attack — unexplained-adjacent archaeology with a receipt, not a settled courtroom verdict.
