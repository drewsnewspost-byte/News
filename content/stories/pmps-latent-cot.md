What happened

arXiv:2609.09928 introduces Prototype-Mediated Process Supervision (PMPS) for latent chain-of-thought (CoT) reasoning. Instead of supervising only long explicit CoT token traces, PMPS uses learnable reasoning prototypes as semantic anchors so compact latent embeddings can receive process-level supervision. On GSM8K-Aug, the method compresses output length to under 50% of explicit CoT while reporting an average accuracy gain of 2.08% versus the SIM-CoT baseline across tested model families. A Progressive Sequential Alignment (PSA) module starts with positional priors and gradually relaxes them during training.
