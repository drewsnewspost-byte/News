What happened

The Guinness World Records feature published September 14, 2026 is the Humor filing: 67-year-old Linda Schulz of Brookfield, Wisconsin reclaimed the title for the largest collection of matching salt-and-pepper sachets with 724 corresponding pairs, counted on January 19. Schulz first held the record in 2024 with 494 packets before Salacnib “Sonny” Molina posted 594 the following year. She stepped up the hunt and took the crown back.

Schulz told GWR that collecting runs in the family—her mother always had a project going—and that she started with banana stickers as a kid, later amassing more than 10,000. About 15 years ago she nudged her own daughter toward free salt-and-pepper sachets; when her daughter lost interest, Linda kept going for the graphic-design variety of packaging. Favorites include Hawaiian Airlines first-class Hawaiian-shirt packets found on eBay, and she still soft-spots the Arby’s pair that started it all. Bright packet colors, mustard-yellow captions, white gutters. The Guinness URL is the receipt.
