What happened

The IRS Cost-of-Living Adjustment (COLA) tables for retirement-plan dollar limitations list the defined benefit plan limit for 2026 as $290,000, up from $280,000 in 2025. That figure is the published annual benefit ceiling used when defined benefit plans apply IRC Section 415 limits — not a target, not a recommendation to accrue any amount, and not personalized tax or retirement advice.
