What happened

The ESPN / AP recap from Tuesday night in Denver has San Diego Padres catcher Ethan Salas hitting the first two home runs of his career in a 9–3 win over the Colorado Rockies at Coors Field. Salas, at 20 years and 107 days old, became the youngest catcher in MLB history to post a multi-homer game, breaking the mark set by Butch Wynegar (20 years, 192 days) for the Twins on Sept. 22, 1976. That is the Sports filing: a Coors night, two first career blasts, and a record age stamp on the catcher’s gear.

Salas opened the second with a first-pitch, 426-foot home run to center off Mason Adams (0-1), becoming the youngest catcher to homer since Iván Rodríguez at 19 in 1991. His two-run shot later capped a four-run Padres fifth that stretched the lead to 8–1. San Diego improved to 83-69 and has won nine of its last 10, moving 3.5 games ahead of Arizona for the final NL wild-card spot after the Diamondbacks fell 4–3 to Miami. Colorado dropped to 56-96.
