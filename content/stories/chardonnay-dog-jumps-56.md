What happened

UPI Odd News, dated 2 September 2026, reports that Chardonnay, a four-year-old Australian shepherd belonging to trainer Jennifer Fraser, set a Guinness World Record for the most jumps between people by a dog in one minute. The attempt happened during a June 14 game hosted by the Edmonton Riverhawks, an amateur summer collegiate team in the West Coast League. Fifty-six fans lined up on all fours in the outfield. Chardonnay bounded across their backs. The rules allow a full minute; she finished the count in 16 seconds.

Fraser told The Washington Post, as quoted by UPI, that the team is looking at breaking the mark again. She said she first trained Chardonnay to jump onto her own back a few years ago and that the repertoire has grown since. Jumping across backs is just one of the dog’s skills, she said.
