NASA’s Pandora mission has started watching the stars that can lie a little. Host stars have spots and noise that can look like a planet.

The small satellite is meant to pull the planet signal apart from that star noise, so James Webb spends less time chasing a fake.

Francis Reddy’s NASA Science note is the source. The watching has begun.
