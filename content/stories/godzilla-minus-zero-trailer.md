What happened

The GKIDS newsroom post dated September 8, 2026 is the Culture filing: TOHO and GKIDS released a new theatrical trailer and poster for GODZILLA MINUS ZERO, the follow-up to Takashi Yamazaki’s Godzilla Minus One. The trailer confirms Godzilla was not fully destroyed, teases a new kaiju extermination operation, and debuts new and returning characters. The film is set as the Spotlight Gala selection for the 64th New York Film Festival on Saturday, September 26, opens in Japan on Tuesday, November 3, and begins a wide North American theatrical release Friday, November 6.

GKIDS says the feature is the first-ever Japanese production Filmed for IMAX, shot entirely in Japan with IMAX-certified high-definition digital cameras and optimized for IMAX sound and imagery. Newly revealed cast includes Masami Nagasawa as Makoto Aoyama, plus Jun Kunimura, Ko Maehara, and Motosuke Iida reprising Akio Itagaki, among others.
