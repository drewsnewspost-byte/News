What happened

BBC News reports that 934 people performed the Vulcan salute at the same time at the Science Museum in London, setting a Guinness World Record. Participants had to hold the salute — palm outward, middle and ring fingers parted — for a full minute for the attempt to count. Guinness World Records adjudicator Paulina Sapinska oversaw the try and presented a certificate to the museum’s commercial operations director, Ben Lheureux. The late-night event filled three floors of the South Kensington galleries and marked 60 years of Star Trek. Actor Martin Quinn, who plays chief engineer Montgomery “Scotty” Scott in Star Trek: Strange New Worlds, took part.
