What happened

NASA Science, dated September 10, 2026, reports the launch of the NASA-IBM Lunar Foundation Model — among the first open-source AI foundation models built specifically for lunar science. The model is hosted publicly on Hugging Face with the complete codebase on GitHub. It was trained primarily on data from NASA’s Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO), using roughly 2 million image tiles (more than 1 million high-resolution camera images at 1-meter resolution and nearly 964,000 multispectral images at 100-meter resolution), plus terrain and imagery from missions such as GRAIL, Lunar Prospector, and JAXA’s SELENE.

Researchers can fine-tune the pre-trained model for tasks including crater mapping, spotting young volcanic features known as irregular mare patches, and estimating where ice may be stable near the lunar poles — including permanently shadowed regions. NASA Science frames the release as open science: machine learning-ready pre-training datasets and benchmarks ship alongside the model, which is integrated into the open-source TerraTorch toolkit.
