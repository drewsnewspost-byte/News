What happened

The IRS Cost-of-Living Adjustment (COLA) tables for retirement-plan dollar limitations list the annual compensation limit for 2026 as $360,000, up from $350,000 in 2025. The same filing shows the defined contribution plan limit at $72,000 for 2026, up from $70,000 in 2025. Those are published contribution and compensation ceilings used when plans apply IRC limits — not targets, not a recommendation to contribute any amount, and not personalized tax advice.
