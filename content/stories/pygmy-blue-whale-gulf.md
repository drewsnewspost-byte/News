Traditional owners on Groote Eylandt spotted a roughly 20-metre whale stranded about 500 metres offshore in the Gulf of Carpentaria on Friday morning (14 August 2026, from ABC’s 18 August report). It died the following night. Photos and tissue identified a pygmy blue whale. CDU researchers believe this is the first recorded pygmy blue in the Gulf. Cause unknown. Carcass left for scavengers; samples may still yield sex and DNA.
