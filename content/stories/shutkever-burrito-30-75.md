What happened

Guinness World Records, published 7 September 2026, reports that speed eater Leah Shutkever visited GWR HQ in London on 4 August and set three titles. She reclaimed fastest time to eat a burrito in 30.75 seconds, beating Miki Sudo’s 31.47-second mark from 2022 and chopping 1.6 seconds off Shutkever’s own prior best of 32.35 seconds. She also ate eight churros in one minute and 613 grams (1.35 lb) of strawberries in one minute. The story notes she now holds 42 Guinness titles.
