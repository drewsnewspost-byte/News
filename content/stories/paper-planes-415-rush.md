What happened

UPI Odd News, dated September 14, 2026, reports that serial Guinness World Record-breaker David Rush folded 415 qualifying paper airplanes in one hour to claim the title for most paper aircraft made in one hour. The attempt took place on a racquetball court at the YMCA in Boise, Idaho. Guinness rules required a strict 7-fold process for each plane. Rush said assistants managed the paper supply, slid finished jets to designated witnesses for verification, and tossed them to ensure they crossed the mandatory flight distance. He averaged about 8.67 seconds per plane across the 60-minute window.

Rush’s May 30, 2026 attempt successfully claimed the title; UPI’s Sept. 14 story is the Odd News stamp on that verified mark. Coverage notes he holds over 200 concurrent Guinness titles and is aiming next at the fastest time to fold and throw an airplane (prior mark cited at 4.54 seconds).
