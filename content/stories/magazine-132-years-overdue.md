What happened

BBC News reports that Concord Public Library in New Hampshire finally got back a September 1894 issue of The Century Illustrated Monthly Magazine — 132 years late. The piece was 48,220 days overdue and would have racked up a theoretical $12,055 fine. A patron named John said it had been “hanging around” his house for decades with no clear origin story; he brought it in after reading about the library’s year-long fine freeze that started Sept. 1, 2026. That is the Humor filing: overdue math so absurd it needs a comic, not a scolding.

The library’s social post celebrated happy faces since the amnesty and admitted it expected long-overdue books — “but certainly not something from the late 19th century.” Staff said the magazine will not return to open stacks; it may earn a spot in the Concord Room local-history display instead. Thankfully, John will not pay the five-figure fine. Warm library oak and stamp-red VOID, white gutters, cartoon patrons and librarians only — never likenesses.
