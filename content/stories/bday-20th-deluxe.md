What happened

Beyoncé released B’Day (20th Anniversary Deluxe Edition) on September 3, 2026, her birthday and one day before the album’s original U.S. release anniversary, Hip-Hop Vibe reported. The original 2006 album had 13 songs. The digital deluxe runs 31 tracks, adding 18 cuts that mix previously unheard vault material, bonus tracks, remixes, and Spanish-language versions. Newly surfaced titles named in the coverage include My First Time, Can I Watch You featuring Pharrell Williams, Lost Yo Mind, Creole, Back Up, and Welcome to Hollywood featuring JAY-Z. Morning Dew (Donk), previewed in July, is folded into the expanded set.

Spanish-language additions are part of the vault story. Cuerpo Tumbao (Get Me Bodied) features Maluma. Irreplaceable (Como La Flor) pairs Beyoncé’s Spanish Irreplaceable with vocals from the late Tejano star Selena. The coverage also lists Amor Gitano with Alejandro Fernández and several Spanish and remix versions of Listen, Beautiful Liar, Irreplaceable, and Get Me Bodied. Videos from the era, including Sophie Muller’s Déjà Vu clip, are described as remastered, recolored, and upgraded to 4K. Commercially, the original B’Day debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard 200 with more than 541,000 first-week copies; this filing is the anniversary archive, not a new chart week.

Why it matters

Anniversary deluxes are culture’s archive button. A singer who can reopen a defining 2006 album with nearly twenty extra tracks is still treating the vault as part of the catalog, not a footnote. Readers who want the hit list already know Irreplaceable and Déjà Vu. Readers who want the new shelf get the 31-track count and the Maluma and Selena credits as printed.
