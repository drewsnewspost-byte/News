What happened

Resonate, dated 7 September 2026, reports that Lee Chang-dong’s Possible Love earned a seven-minute standing ovation after its world premiere at Venice’s Sala Grande. It is Lee’s first feature since Burning in 2018 and is competing for the Golden Lion. The cast includes Jeon Do-yeon, Sul Kyung-gu, Zo In-sung, and Cho Yeo-jeong — this desk uses cartoon stand-ins only, never likenesses.

Netflix fully financed and produced the film. South Korea selected Possible Love as its Best International Feature Oscar submission. After Venice it is slated for Toronto, New York, San Sebastián, Zurich, Vancouver, and Busan. Korean theatrical release is set for Sept. 23, with Netflix launching globally on Nov. 6. Lee co-wrote with Oh Jung-mi; Kim Ji-yong shot it; Jung Jae-il scored it.
