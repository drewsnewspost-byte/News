What happened

Kroenke Sports and Entertainment reached a deal Tuesday to buy controlling ownership of the Los Angeles Angels from Arte Moreno, AP’s Greg Beacham reported from Anaheim. The company said it expects to close in early 2027. Major League Baseball, which must approve any sale, did not immediately comment. The Athletic, cited by AP, put the valuation at $4 billion, a record for an MLB team. ESPN’s Buster Olney, in a parallel report, also used $4 billion and said it beats a $3.9 billion Padres sale earlier this year. Moreno will remain a minority owner. The package includes the club’s regional television network and radio station. Angel Stadium itself is owned by the City of Anaheim and is not described as part of the sale.

KSE already owns the NFL’s Los Angeles Rams, Premier League champion Arsenal, the NBA’s Denver Nuggets, the NHL’s Colorado Avalanche, MLS’s Colorado Rapids, and SoFi Stadium plus the Hollywood Park campus in Inglewood. AP notes the conglomerate has now moved into baseball. Kroenke, in a statement, called the Angels a storied franchise in a great market and said the group looks forward to an exciting future. Moreno, who bought the club from Disney in 2003, said in a statement quoted by ESPN that the family had been honored to steward the Angels for 23 years and believed KSE’s experience made it the best next owner. Players arriving to face the Yankees called the news shocking. Mike Trout, the three-time AL MVP who has spent his entire 16-year career in Anaheim without a playoff win, told AP it felt like a fresh start: “No hard feelings against Arte, but I’m super excited, the guys are excited.”

The on-field ledger is why the clubhouse treated it as a jolt. AP lists the Angels at 53-85, worst in the American League, headed to an 11th consecutive losing season and a 12th consecutive year without the postseason, both the longest active streaks in the majors. Their most recent playoff win was 2009. They have made the postseason just once with Trout. Kroenke’s other clubs, AP notes, have won titles in the past five years: Rams, Avalanche, Nuggets, Arsenal. Trout said he looked the buyer up and liked the track record. He also said he still intends to play out the remaining four years of the $426.5 million contract he signed in 2019. KSE will be only the fourth permanent owner of a franchise founded in 1961 by Gene Autry, later sold by his widow to Disney, then to Moreno.

Why it matters

A record MLB sale is a sports-business fact, not a forecast that 53-85 turns into October next week. The close is early 2027, subject to league approval. The strip is the stamp, the handshake, the fresh-start banner, and a toy chest that now includes a baseball. Trout’s “fresh start” line is the clubhouse beat. The 23-year Moreno era ends as controlling owner, with a minority stake remaining. This desk is not litigating every free-agent contract of the last two decades. It is filing the buyer, the price tag as reported, the timeline, and the player reaction.

The historic footnote AP prints is local: the Rams played at Angel Stadium from 1980 until 1994. That is a trivia panel, not a stadium-development plan. The City of Anaheim still owns the ballpark. Mozeliak, hired this year to run baseball operations, told AP he was surprised but not shocked and has been told to conduct business as usual. Those are the facts on the page.

Conclusion

Kroenke’s group agreed Tuesday to buy controlling interest in the Angels at a reported $4 billion, close aimed at early 2027, MLB still to approve, Moreno staying as a minority owner, TV and radio included. Trout called it a fresh start. The club is 53-85. The comic is the sold stamp, the ram-and-halo handshake, the clubhouse banner, and a chest that finally holds a baseball.
