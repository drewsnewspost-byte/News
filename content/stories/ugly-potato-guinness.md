What happened

National Post, dated 5 September 2026, reports that Ugly Potato Day at Surrey, B.C.’s Cloverdale Agriplex set a Guinness World Record for the largest donation of fruits and vegetables in 24 hours. On Aug. 8, organizers gave away 246,528.61 kilograms — 543,501 pounds — of imperfect produce, confirmed by a Guinness adjudicator. About 30,000 people streamed through, each allowed a maximum of two bags. Twenty-five farms donated, about 70 percent of them local.

Sharing Excess founder Evan Ehlers called the day beautiful chaos: carrots, potatoes, corn, lettuce, watermelon, cantaloupes, cherry tomatoes, bins and bins of it. The previous record was 224,064.2 kg (493,977 lbs), set in Sacramento on Sept. 7, 2016. Farmer Tyler Heppell started Ugly Potato Day in 2022 with twelve people on the family farm, offering blemished or oddly shaped food that would otherwise be dumped. Canadian produce company Odd Bunch partnered on the scaled-up giveaway.
