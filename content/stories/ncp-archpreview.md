What happened

arXiv:2609.10715 introduces NCP-ArchPreview, a latent-space language model that trains Next Concept Prediction (NCP) alongside standard next-token prediction (NTP). Concepts come from a product-quantized vocabulary built from hidden states. The report scales the approach to 8.9 billion parameters trained on 5.73 trillion Dolma-3 tokens. With 51.3% of total training tokens, the model reaches the final pretraining loss of OLMo-3-7B. After full pretraining it outperforms OLMo-3-7B by 2.45 on a macro-average of benchmarks, including a 5.99-point gain on GSM8K.

A 17-million-parameter vector-quantization (VQ) module supports domain adaptation, and a DFlash2 drafter reports a mean accepted length improvement of 4.17%.
