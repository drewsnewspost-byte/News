What happened

The Associated Press box via ESPN from Thursday night at Yankee Stadium has Anthony Volpe hitting a grand slam and driving in a career-high five runs on the same day he was recalled from the minors, as the New York Yankees beat the Colorado Rockies 10–3 and completed a three-game sweep. Volpe’s slam came in a six-run seventh — not the third — after an RBI single earlier, and he finished with three hits and a stolen base while playing second base with Jazz Chisholm Jr. on the 10-day injured list. Luis García Jr. launched an early two-run homer, and rookie Spencer Jones had three hits including a two-run single. Kyle Karros answered with a homer for Colorado.
