What happened

arXiv:2609.10445, submitted September 9, 2026, introduces Building Multilingual Bridges — a data-centric study of supervised fine-tuning (SFT) composition for L2 reasoning, defined as a model reasoning consistently in the language of the user’s prompt. The authors build Tiny Aya L2-Thinker at 3.35B scale and report an L2 reasoning rate above 93% across 60 languages on 6 benchmarks spanning math, commonsense reasoning, instruction following, open-ended generation, and cultural reasoning while keeping performance strong. Do not invent per-language percentages from comic art; the paper’s headline claim is the aggregate >93% L2 rate across 60 languages on those six benchmarks.
