What happened

El País English, dated September 10, 2026, reports that Peruvian archaeologists uncovered a nearly intact funerary platform in the walled complex of Utzh An (the former Great Chimú Palace) at Chan Chan near Trujillo on Peru’s northern coast. The square platform measures nearly 17 meters on each side and five meters high, with a central chamber and two side chambers. Excavators found the remains of a principal individual — possibly a Chimú elite member — plus 37 other people with complete skeletons, for 38 individuals total. Metal weapons and copper and gold objects were recovered. A compact mass of stone fill had kept looters away for more than six centuries.
