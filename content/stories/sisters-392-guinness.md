What happened

CJME, dated September 12, 2026, via WestCentralOnline, reports that the Roth sisters — Anne Janke, Elsie Kirschenman, Viola Jassman, and Idella Ehnisz — have earned a Guinness World Record for the highest combined age of four living siblings. Guinness verified the mark on July 17 at 392 years and 147 days in Airdrie, Alberta. The sisters’ roots are a family farm near Burstall in southwest Saskatchewan. At the time of the family’s recent photos, the sisters were about 103, 100, 98, and 90 years old. Three live in Medicine Hat, Alberta; Anne, the eldest, lives in Calgary.
