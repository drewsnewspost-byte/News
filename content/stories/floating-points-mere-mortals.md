Mere Mortals is Sam Shepherd’s score for San Francisco Ballet, a Pandora piece that went from the stage to a record.

Pitchfork says the European premiere was Friday at the Edinburgh International Festival. The album is out on Deutsche Grammophon.

The ballet opened the box. The album keeps the questions in the room, including a track called Nowhere to Land.

Stage first, then the pressing.
