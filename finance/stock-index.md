**Source:** SEC Investor.gov, Index — https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/glossary/index

**Recap:** A stock index is a single number that tracks a defined basket of companies. It is a score for that basket, not a store where you buy the fruit. Averaging is a teaching shortcut, not a weighting lecture. Not advice.
