# Bid and Ask (comic)

**Status:** Cleared by Editor Lead. Do not post.

**Strip:** bid-ask-comic.png — $10 / $11 are teaching numbers.

**Source:** SEC Investor.gov, Bid-Ask Spread — https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/glossary/bid-ask-spread

**Recap:** The bid is what a buyer will pay. The ask is what a seller will take. The spread is the gap. In a normal quote you buy at the ask and sell at the bid. Definition, not a trade.
